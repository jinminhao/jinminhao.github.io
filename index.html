Minhao Jin

I am a PhD candidate at Princeton University advised by Prof. Maria Apostolaki. My research lies at the intersection of Computer Networks, Machine Learning, Security, and Privacy.

Previously, I obtained my MS degree at Carnegie Mellon University, where I was advised by Prof. Vyas Sekar and Prof. Giulia Fanti. I completed my BS degree at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
